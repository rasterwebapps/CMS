How to plan this term's timetable

  1. Pick the term and cohort above, choose whether to plan against enrolled headcount or sanctioned intake (useful in a first term where enrollment is still rolling in), and Calculate.
  2. In Cohort Room Allocation's Theory Sections step: if no single classroom fits the whole cohort, you'll be walked through splitting it into sections, each with its own classroom.
  3. In the Lab/Clinical steps, pick a subject with Lab/Clinical hours and a default venue — one row auto-generates per section; Split a row if a venue can't seat the whole section — then Commit.
  4. Open Skeleton Builder to place Theory/Lab/Clinical sessions into periods for each subject.
  5. Open Staffing to assign faculty — Theory rooms are picked there, but Lab/Clinical rooms are already fixed from Cohort Room Allocation and can't be changed here.
